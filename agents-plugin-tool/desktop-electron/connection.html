Connect to your dashboard

Werkschaft connects to a healthy local dashboard or starts its bundled daemon automatically on Windows and macOS.

Choose Connection → Retry (Ctrl+R on Windows, ⌘R on macOS) to try connecting and starting the daemon again.

Closing the window does not explicitly shut down the daemon. If Windows refuses job breakaway, the daemon may end when its containing launcher or terminal job closes. Replacement is available through Connection → Replace daemon (same core).
