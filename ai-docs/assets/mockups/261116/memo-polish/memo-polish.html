Memo pad polish

Variant State

Variants

  • Current - memo.css as shipped: 320px, olive pad (hue 24%) behind olive notes (hue 40%), dark textareas with resize grips, bare ✕ / Undo.
  • A Quiet - 440px. Neutral pad (--wb-card) with a thin warm top edge; notes are dark muted cards with a 3px warm left strip. Lowest contrast against the rest of the dashboard; the post-it feel is a hint, not a surface.
  • B Paper - 440px. Neutral pad; notes are muted amber paper with a darker adhesive strip and a folded bottom-right corner, warm ink. Clear post-it identity, still dark.
  • C Board - 480px. Same paper as B laid out on a 2-column grid; memos longer than ~90 chars or 3 lines and the note being edited span both columns. Most "wall of stickies", weakest for long text and reading order.

Shared polish (A/B/C)

  • Textareas are chrome-less and auto-grow (resize: none); the add box carries the border/focus ring, so there are no resize grips.
  • Header: memo icon, "Memos", workspace as a mono chip (ellipsized; full path stays in title), memo count, 28px close button.
  • Key hints (Enter save, Shift+Enter newline) move out of the placeholder into a kbd row inside the add box, dimmed when idle and full-strength on focus; the row is always laid out so focus/blur never shifts the list. A note being edited shows the same row plus Esc cancel. The placeholder shrinks to "Add a memo…".
  • Delete ✕ fades in on note hover/focus and turns danger-red on its own hover. Undo is a bottom snackbar instead of a trailing text line.
  • Korean: word-break: keep-all + overflow-wrap: anywhere (whole-word wrapping, long URLs still break), line-height 1.6, 13.5px.
  • No timestamps: the memo record is only {id, text} (wsdashboard/memos.go).
