# Consolidate conversation mail delivery and report presentation

## Background

The dashboard can show a structured mailbox-history row and a separate mailbox wake card for the same delivery. They represent different observations: the mail exchange and the text delivered to the model. Their simultaneous display is not evidence of duplicate model delivery. Some child reports also appear as owner/whiteboard input because producer paths share input records.

The owner approved one ticket with two phases under (781)=a: a mockup-first, T0 asset/display-only Phase 1, followed by an optional, separately settled Phase 2 for delivery IDs and typed reports. MAIN relayed (804)=ok on 2026-11-14 at 22:42:15 UTC, approving the Phase 1 mockup and authorizing ticket finalization. This capture preserves that settled baseline; it does not authorize implementation or claim browser acceptance.

The supporting rendering inventory was a static investigation, not approval of its entire five-family consolidation proposal. MAIN's 2026-11-14 23:23:53 UTC relay supplies the owner-approved (811)/(812) unified chat/mailbox/whiteboard mockup and settlements (826), (827) and (828). This explicitly extends the display-only Phase 1 baseline with peer-lead mail presentation and whiteboard-update cards/navigation, while retaining the original mail-correlation safeguards and the optional, separately settled Phase 2 boundary. The approved visual extension is not implementation or runtime acceptance.

## Decisions

1. **Keep one ticket with two stable phases — owner-confirmed (781)=a.** Phase 1 remains T0 display-only, including the explicitly approved unified visual extension relayed under (826)–(828). Phase 2 remains optional and deferred pending its own explicit decisions and compatibility assessment. Rejected: treating all rendering families or producer/schema changes as part of Phase 1.
2. **Keep both approved visual references — (804)=ok and (826)=ok.** Preserve `ai-docs/tickets/assets/261115-feat-ws-dashboard-conversation-rendering-consolidation/approved-phase1-mockup.html` unchanged, alongside the approved unified HTML and four theme/width captures linked below. Historical “not approved” or proposal annotations remain part of the immutable reference bytes; the approval provenance and settled rules in this ticket supersede those drafting annotations. Synthetic names, times, case headings, explanatory side notes and the 360 px preview are review fixtures, not production copy or an approved product breakpoint.
3. **Give matched mail one primary representation.** Preserve the familiar mail arrow, peer, preview and time. When a corresponding mail row can be reliably identified from existing data, subordinate the wake's raw transport text under that row in a collapsed “Delivery details” disclosure rather than another standalone wake card. The owner-facing label may be “전달 세부”. Keep mail-body and delivery-detail expansion independent.
4. **Retain honest fallbacks.** When the matching mail row or reliable correlation evidence is missing, retain an independent compact notification with expandable delivery details. History retention, pagination or an unavailable body is not permission to discard the remaining wake detail. Rejected: blanket suppression of `wake/mail`, matching by body equality alone, timestamp proximity alone, or the presence of some nearby mail.
5. **Preserve legitimate repetition and timing distinctions.** Equal sender/body text can belong to separate messages. Keep those messages independent. Mail claim/history time is not model-consumption time; grouping must not fabricate delivery acknowledgment or change observed timestamps.
6. **Make report labels follow available provenance.** Display an already-identifiable child report distinctly from actual owner/whiteboard input. Preserve genuine human input as human input. For an inseparable legacy/mixed record whose uncertainty is supported by existing data, use a neutral source-unresolved presentation and retain the original text; do not fabricate a split. A prose marker such as `[child report]` is not proof of authorship. New producer metadata or typed-report emission belongs outside Phase 1.
7. **Phase 1 compatibility target is T0, asset/display-only.** Change presentation without changing stored records, mailbox queue/claim/acknowledgment behavior, lead-input text, producer event contracts or API semantics. No schema/protocol bump or new persisted field is authorized. A need to cross that boundary returns to MAIN before implementation.
8. **Defer the optional Phase 2 mechanisms.** Delivery IDs and typed reports are the owner-approved subjects for possible later work, not selected APIs or event/store designs. Their exact fields, correlation rules, producer changes, compatibility tier and execution scope remain unselected. Avoid T2; any T2 proposal requires explicit owner approval under the compatibility rules. This ticket does not supply an executable Phase 2 plan.
9. **Accept the unified visual direction — (826)=ok.** In the same conversation, preserve the approved mail row presentation and show a green “peer lead” badge (the approved Korean UI wording is “피어 리드”) when the stored first line matches the existing `[ws note: from a peer lead session ...]` note. This is display-only parsing of existing text, not new trusted identity/privilege metadata or proof that arbitrary prose establishes authorship. Keep the original note inspectable in raw delivery details. Include whiteboard-update cards with topic bars, L-marked log lines, panel recap plus board link, and items with options preview and the board-answer action. The (812) current-board-look section with numbered pins is an approved component reference, not authority to redesign or translate the board.
10. **Use the settled fold rule — (827)=a.** A whiteboard update containing exactly one topic and log lines only uses the one-line folded form. Any panel, item or multiple topics requires the full update card. Do not substitute an unconfirmed text-length threshold or restrict the rule to the mockup's single-log example.
11. **Keep panels out of the chat body — (828)=ok.** Show only the panel's one-line recap and board link such as `#pN`; no in-place panel expansion or panel HTML belongs in chat. Preserve the separate committed-effects constraint from `261115-feat-ws-whiteboard-committed-effects-in-conversation`. Translating the board's existing English labels is a separate item, not part of this capture.
12. **Navigate update cards to the board — owner requirement relayed with (826)–(828).** Clicking a whiteboard-update card must navigate to that topic's card on the board. This is a required behavior, not merely a decorative link. Preserve independent disclosure and existing scroll/board ownership rather than turning content expansion into an unintended navigation action.
13. **Evaluate exact-element navigation on a best-effort basis — same owner requirement.** Clicking an individual log line, panel or item should reach that exact board element where feasible, rather than stopping at its topic card. Inspect existing immutable-entry, stable-panel and item anchors, then record the selected implementation approach and its tested limits in this ticket before claiming element-level support. No new anchor/wire/store mechanism is settled by this requirement, and unavailable, removed or unresolvable elements must not be reported as successfully reached. Exact-element coverage is best-effort; the required topic-card navigation remains independently verifiable.

- Respect the separately approved asset-split start pause and consume the landed split's move map before a new UI implementation starts. The split's overlap does not authorize source import or bypass its coordination gate.
- Read `ai-docs/manuals/shipped-surface-boundary.md` before eventual tooling/asset edits, and `ai-docs/manuals/release-compatibility.md` for any eventual cross-version surface change. Phase 1 does not authorize shipped playbook edits.

## Phases

### Phase 1: Consolidate mail details, source labels and approved unified update cards

Implement the approved display-only baseline and (826)–(828) extension in the existing conversation rendering paths after the asset-split start pause is cleared and separate execution is authorized. Use both tracked approved HTML references and the four unified theme/width captures. Preserve one canonical mail representation for safely correlated records, subordinate raw delivery diagnostics, compact unmatched fallbacks, independent repeated messages, and source labels that do not misrepresent machine reports as owner input.

Add the display-only stored-note peer-lead badge and approved whiteboard-update presentation: topic bars, L log lines, panel recap plus board link only, and items with option previews/board-answer navigation. Apply exactly the one-topic/log-only folded rule; panels, items or multiple topics use the full card. Clicking a per-topic update card must reach that topic card on the board. Review and implement exact log/panel/item targeting where feasible using validated existing anchors/history-loading paths, then record the chosen approach, supported cases and limitations in this ticket. Best-effort element targeting must not weaken required topic-card navigation or fabricate successful targeting of unavailable elements.

Keep all stored events and lead-input text unchanged. Reuse existing data and presentation substrates; no new delivery identity, metadata producer or schema is selected by this phase. Do not import panel HTML into chat, change the committed-effects producer contract, or turn the optional Phase 2 into a prerequisite by inventing new metadata. Return an actual data/compatibility gap to MAIN before widening scope.

Verification must cover:

- A reliably matched mail/wake pair with collapsed details and with expanded mail body/delivery diagnostics; the raw text remains inspectable without another primary wake card.
- Missing history, expired body, unmatched/ambiguous records, historical pagination and reconnect, preserving the compact fallback and preventing orphaned or duplicated diagnostics as entries arrive.
- Two separate messages with identical sender/body, distinct senders with equal bodies, and returned/reclaimed history, without content-based deletion or invented delivery acknowledgment.
- An identifiable child report distinct from real human input, and an inseparable legacy/mixed example retaining its original text and honest uncertainty without prose-based provenance inference.
- Initial render and live/history insertion, disclosure-state retention, stable entry identity/cursors and existing scroll behavior.
- Side-by-side result captures against the unchanged `approved-phase1-mockup.html` for its six baseline cases, and against `811-812-unified-chat-mockup.html` plus all four approved light/dark 1280/360 PNGs for unified cases. - Peer-lead stored-note match and absent/malformed-note fallbacks, retaining the escaped original note in delivery details without changing identity, trust, input text or producer data.
- One topic with one or several log lines folded; panels, items and multiple topics displayed as full cards. Verify recap-only panels and board-answer links with no chat panel HTML or in-place expansion.
- Required update-card-to-topic navigation independently from best-effort exact log/panel/item navigation. Test each supported element type, folded/paged/lazy history, multiple topics, removed/missing targets, keyboard activation and the existing board/scroll ownership rules. Record the chosen validated-anchor approach and unsupported cases; do not equate opening a topic with reaching the requested element.
